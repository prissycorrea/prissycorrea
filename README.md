## 👩‍💻 About me

I'm a **Software Engineer at [Avanade](https://www.avanade.com/pt-br)** with **4+ years in front-end**, building for a client in the healthcare sector and creating internal portals and automations. Before tech, I spent **6 years as a teacher**, and that still shapes how I communicate, mentor and work with people.

- 🌐 Accessible, responsive and SEO-friendly interfaces with **React, Next.js and TypeScript**
- 🏢 Microsoft 365 solutions with **SharePoint, PnP PowerShell, Power Platform and Copilot Studio**
- 🤖 **Python and PowerShell** automations, web scraping and Chrome extensions
- 👩‍🏫 Technical mentor and internal instructor of **GitHub Copilot** and **React** trainings
- 🎓 Post-grad in **Front-End Engineering** at [FIAP](https://www.fiap.com.br/) · currently learning **Swift** 🍎
